I work at the intersection of **self-evolving agents**, **multimodal foundation models**, and **visual content generation** — connecting research ideas to open-source systems and to products people actually use.

At MSRA I lead the **recursive self-improvement (RSI)** research direction, spanning agent harnesses, tasks and rewards, data pipelines, and model training, and extending toward **AI4AI**: using AI systems to improve AI research and development itself. I am also a long-term contributor to Microsoft's first-party multimodal models, including **Phi-3.5 Vision**, **Phi-4-mini**, and the next-generation **MAI** model series.

**40+ peer-reviewed papers** (20+ as first or corresponding author) at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, and AAAI · **10+ international patents** as primary inventor · Technology transferred into GitHub Copilot, Azure AI Foundry, and Office.

## Featured open-source work

### Self-evolving agents

- **[SkillOpt](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillOpt)** — Trains reusable natural-language skills for frozen LLM agents through trajectory-driven optimization and validation-gated updates. Best or tied-best across **all 52 evaluated settings**. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/SkillOpt?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillOpt/stargazers)
- **[Resource2Skill](https://github.com/microsoft/Resource2Skill)** — Distills tutorials, videos, code, and other human-created multimodal resources into reusable, executable agent skills. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/Resource2Skill?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/Resource2Skill/stargazers)
- **[SkillLens](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillLens)** — A systematic study of how model-generated skills are produced, consumed, and reused across the skill lifecycle. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/SkillLens?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillLens/stargazers)

### Multimodal learning and generation

- **[LLM2CLIP](https://github.com/microsoft/LLM2CLIP)** — Uses large language models as textual teachers to improve vision-language representation learning. **[AAAI 2026 Outstanding Paper Award](https://aaai.org/about-aaai/aaai-awards/aaai-conference-paper-awards-and-recognition/)** and **WAIC 2026 Youth Outstanding Paper Nomination Award**; **[300K+ Hugging Face downloads](https://huggingface.co/collections/microsoft/llm2clip-672323a266173cfa40b32d4c)**, and the technique now serves as the visual-pretraining approach for Phi-4-mini. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/LLM2CLIP?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/LLM2CLIP/stargazers)
- **[World-R1](https://github.com/microsoft/World-R1)** — Reinforces 3D constraints in text-to-video generation with camera-aware initialization and 3D-aware rewards. **ICML 2026.** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/World-R1?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/World-R1/stargazers)
- **[Latent Spatial Memory](https://github.com/microsoft/LatentSpatialMemory)** — Persistent latent 3D scene memory for video world models: **10.57× faster generation** and **55× lower 3D-cache memory**. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/LatentSpatialMemory?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/LatentSpatialMemory/stargazers)
- **[RAS](https://github.com/microsoft/RAS)** — Region-adaptive, training-free sampling for efficient diffusion-transformer inference. **CVPR 2026.** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/RAS?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/RAS/stargazers)

### Benchmarks and evaluation

- **[BizGenEval](https://github.com/microsoft/BizGenEval)** — A systematic benchmark for commercial visual content generation, now used as a reward and evaluation system in Microsoft PowerPoint. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/BizGenEval?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/BizGenEval/stargazers)
- **[AVGen-Bench](https://github.com/microsoft/AVGen-Bench)** — Task-driven, multi-granular evaluation for text-to-audio-video generation. [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/microsoft/AVGen-Bench?style=flat-square&label=stars)](https://github.com/microsoft/AVGen-Bench/stargazers)
